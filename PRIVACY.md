# Privacy Policy — Tukka Meter (SSC / Bank / Railway)

Last updated: [DATE]

Tukka Meter is a free mock-test tracking tool. This page explains what data
it accesses and how.

## Data stored on your device

By default, all your mock scores, exam selections, and settings are stored
only in your browser's local storage, on your own device. This app has no
backend server, no account system, and no database — nothing you enter is
ever sent to me or to any server I control.

## Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose to turn on "Sync with Google Drive," the app asks Google for
permission to create and update a single small backup file **that it creates
itself** in your own Google Drive, using Google's `drive.file` scope. This
scope means the app can only ever see files it created — it cannot see,
list, or modify anything else already in your Drive.

- This backup file is stored in your own Google account, not on any server
  operated by me.
- I (the developer) never receive, see, or have access to this data at any
  point.
- You can disconnect at any time from the app's Cloud Sync settings.
- You can permanently delete the backup file from your Drive at any time
  using the "Delete Cloud Backup" button in the same settings panel.

## Data you export or share manually

The app lets you manually export a backup file (JSON) to your device, or
share it via your device's native share sheet (e.g. to WhatsApp, email, or
another app of your choosing). These actions are entirely user-initiated —
the app does not send this data anywhere on its own.

## Third parties

This app does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising of any kind. The
only third party involved is Google, and only if you explicitly opt in to
Drive sync, solely to let you save/restore your own backup file in your own
account.
