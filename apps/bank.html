Backup & Restore

Save a copy of your data, or bring it back from a saved file.

Export as Backup Data

This saves mocks and progress from everything you track across SSC, Bank, Railway & the CGL Maths Tracker — not just the exam or category you have open right now — into one file, like taking a photo of everything you've logged so far. Keep this file safe (Google Drive, email to yourself, etc.) so you never lose your data — even if you clear your browser or switch devices.

Import from Backup File

Already have a backup file? Use this to bring all that data back — across every Bank exam in the file — into the app, useful after reinstalling, switching devices, or if something got accidentally deleted. Note: this replaces whatever is currently in the app for each exam included in the file, so make sure it's the right file.

Cloud Sync (Google Drive)

Sign in with your Google account to back up all your data — SSC, Bank, Railway & the CGL Maths Tracker together — to Google Drive in one file. Connect once from any one of them and it covers all four; sign in with that same account on any other device — PC, tablet, or phone — and everything carries over automatically. Completely optional, the app still works fully offline.

  • Backup is saved privately to your own Google Drive
  • Switch or reinstall on any device without losing progress
  • Nothing is uploaded until you tap "Sync with Google Drive"

There's no server behind this app — your data goes straight to your own Google Drive and is never seen or stored by anyone else, including us. That's also why Google asks you to reconfirm sign-in every hour instead of trusting it forever — a quick tap on "Sync Now" keeps that privacy guarantee in place.
